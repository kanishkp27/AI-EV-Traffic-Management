## 💰 Pricing in Indian Rupees (INR)

| Charger Type | Rate | Use Case |
|-------------|------|----------|
| **Level 1** (120V) | ₹8.50/kWh | Slow home charging |
| **Level 2** (240V) | ₹12.00/kWh | Standard charging |
| **DC Fast** | ₹15.00/kWh | Expressway stations |
| **Residential** | ₹7.00/kWh | Home installations |

### Example Cost Calculation:
```
Trip: Delhi to Jaipur (240 km)
Battery Capacity: 75 kWh
Energy Consumed: 240 × 0.15 = 36 kWh
DC Fast Charging Cost: 36 × ₹15 = ₹540
Cost per km: ₹2.25/km
```
